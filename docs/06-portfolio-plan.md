## What was re-derived away

- **Acts II–III are cut, including the $4 hearing.** The page's strongest material is all
  Act I: the intake question, the `0`, the judgment document, the refusal. The two-person
  loop is told with ONE still — the arrival screen, which renders today after minting one
  invitation (one server call, $0) — plus one sentence of prose. The L1/v2 story becomes
  "and when she answers, it re-hears with two blind advocates and a swap gate — tested,
  gated, waiting" — which is true, unit- and golden-tested (14/14), and does not require
  having burned $4 to say. If a real L1 artifact is ever wanted, it is one command and $4
  later; nothing here forecloses it.
- **The design pass is two surfaces, not five.** The judgment page already CONTAINS most
  beats — the `0`, the level-as-refusal framing ("only one side has spoken"), the document,
  `self_only`, limits — so designing it well produces most captures at once. The second
  surface is intake's what-do-you-want question. Everything else gets a consistency sweep
  limited to what appears in frame.
- **The refusal beat and the `0` beat are the same surface** (the judgment page's "Before
  you read it" block). No separate refusal screen is designed.
- Share screen restyle, decline branch, recording, mobile verification, runbook work: gone.
